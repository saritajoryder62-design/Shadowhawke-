Our story

Our untold story

Our roots go back further than 2014, to working with one of Rotorua’s local youth camps. That experience sparked something, and Shadowhawk Equine was born.

  1. 2014 Established as a small, term-time riding school.
  2. Growing More whānau joined, and holiday programmes followed.
  3. Listening We saw a gap in support for young people’s emotional wellbeing, nervous-system regulation and real human connection, and chose to respond rather than step away.
  4. Today With training in psychology, counselling and mental health, we blend equine-assisted therapy, mindfulness, breathwork and experiential learning for tamariki, rangatahi and adults.
“Horses are not tools. They are partners in learning and growth.”

Questions

Good to know

Does my child need riding experience?

No. All experience levels are welcome. Every rider gets an individual learning plan and a certificate for each milestone.

How old do riders need to be?

equiNZone After School is for ages 8 and up, Break Away Camps for ages 11 and up, and the Hōtaka Rangatahi Youth Programme for ages 13 to 24. Assuage Equine Therapy is for tamariki, rangatahi and adults.

What should they wear?

Closed-toe shoes and long pants. All riding gear is supplied.

How big are the groups?

equiNZone groups have 5 to 6 riders with 2 instructors. Break Away Camps take a maximum of 10.

Is pick-up and drop-off available?

Yes. For equiNZone After School, pick-up and drop-off is an extra $10.

How do I book?

Send an enquiry with the form below, call us on 021 628 274 or email shadowhawkequine@gmail.com, and we’ll be in touch to arrange a first session.
